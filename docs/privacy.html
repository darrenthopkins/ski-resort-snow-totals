Lift Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-03
App name: Lift: Ski Day Planner
Developer: Darren Hopkins

Summary

  • No account required
  • No tracking
  • No selling of personal data
  • Optional location is used to calculate proximity to ski resorts

Information We Collect

Data You Provide

Lift does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to submit personal information.

Location Data (Optional)

If you grant location permission, Lift may access your device location to:

  • estimate distance to ski resorts
  • sort resorts by proximity

Location is used to provide app functionality. Lift does not sell your location data.

If you deny location permission, Lift will still function using a default regional location.

Automatically Collected Data

Lift does not intentionally collect personal identifiers (such as name, email, phone number) and does not perform cross-app tracking.

How We Use Information

If location access is granted, Lift uses it only to provide proximity-based features (distance and sorting).

Lift uses third-party data sources for weather/snow information to compute and display planning information.

Data Sharing

Lift does not sell personal data.

Lift may request data from third-party services to retrieve weather/snow information. Those providers may receive standard request metadata (for example, IP address) as part of normal internet communication.

Data Retention

Lift does not maintain user accounts and does not store personal data on developer-controlled servers.

Any on-device cached data is used to improve performance and may be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.

Children’s Privacy

Lift is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. Lift does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We aim to minimize data handling. Where network requests are used to retrieve public weather/snow data, standard transport security (HTTPS) is used when available.

Your Choices

  • You may deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You may uninstall the app to remove on-device cached data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above indicates the latest revision.
